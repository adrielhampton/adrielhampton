The O2O gap in progressive organizing

Every funder, every network leader, every field director is asking the same question right now: how do we move people from screens to streets? The term for it is online-to-offline organizing — O2O — and it's the central strategic challenge for Democrats heading into the 2026 midterms.

The problem is that most digital acquisition programs deliver email addresses and nothing else. An email is a broadcast channel. It doesn't knock on a door. It doesn't show up to a community meeting. It doesn't turn out for a rally in a DCCC battleground district. A phone number does all of those things.

What makes AHG different

The Adriel Hampton Group runs Meta lead ad campaigns that capture both email and phone number simultaneously on every signup. This isn't a feature we bolt on — it's the core of how our campaigns are built. Every lead we deliver is O2O-ready from the moment it hits your CRM.

M&R's 2025 Benchmarks report a $3.20 cost per lead on Meta for large national nonprofits — but their framework only measures email list growth. AHG tracks a different metric: O2O lead cost — the cost to acquire a contact with both email and phone, ready for same-day offline follow-up. Our O2O lead cost runs $3–5, near parity with the M&R benchmark despite capturing the additional data that makes field organizing possible.

We've also proven that bilingual campaigns work. In a recent Spanish-language tenant organizing campaign, we drove cost per lead down 74% — from $5.38 to $1.40 — by optimizing form-level friction rather than creative. That's a finding most agencies miss entirely, because they only look at the ad, not the form.

How it works

AHG builds Meta lead campaigns around your organization's existing data and audiences, then optimizes daily toward the metrics that matter for offline organizing.

01
Audience Architecture

We use your CRM data, Facebook followers, and voter file matches to build lookalike audiences that overcome Meta's restrictions on ideological targeting. Your owned data is the most powerful targeting asset on the platform.

02
Multivariate Creative Testing

Every campaign launches with multiple image, headline, and body text variants. Meta's algorithm promotes the strongest combinations. We learn which narratives land with which demographics — and adjust in real time.

03
In-App Lead Capture — Email + Phone

Leads sign up directly inside Facebook and Instagram using native forms with autofill. No landing pages to lose people on. Every form captures name, email, phone, and zip — the full O2O data set.

04
Real-Time CRM Sync and Follow-Up

Leads flow into your CRM (Action Network, EveryAction, NationBuilder) via Zapier within minutes. Your field team can call or text new supporters the same day they sign up — when motivation is highest.
