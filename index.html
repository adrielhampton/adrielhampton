Founder, The Adriel Hampton Group · Carlsbad, CA

I'm a digital acquisition strategist with over a decade of experience running paid social campaigns for progressive organizations. AHG is a boutique consultancy — I work directly with every client. No account manager handoffs. No junior staff on your campaign.

My specialty is Meta lead ads for member-mobilization organizations: the campaigns that need to put real people on phones, at doors, and in rooms. The dual email + phone capture model I've built is designed specifically for organizations that measure success by offline action, not just list size.

I'm a 2025 Reed Award winner for digital excellence in political and advocacy campaigning. My work has been covered by MIT Technology Review, NBC, CNN, NPR, and the BBC. I'm a citizen of the Chickasaw Nation and the founder of Conscious Fathers, a community resource and podcast for engaged dads.
