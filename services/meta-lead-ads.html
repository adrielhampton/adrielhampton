How It Works

From scroll to organizer call in four steps.

01
Campaign Strategy & Audience Build

We define the target audience — age, geography, issue interest — and build the Meta campaign architecture. Separate ad sets for English and Spanish where appropriate.

02
Creative & Form Design

We develop ad creative and build the native lead form — the form that lives inside Facebook and Instagram. Short, frictionless, pre-filled where Meta allows. Email + phone, every time.

03
Launch, Monitor & Optimize

We launch, watch CPL daily, kill underperforming creative quickly, and scale what works. When CTR is strong but CPL is high, we dig into form completion — that's almost always where the problem lives.

04
CRM Delivery & Suppression

Leads sync to your CRM or Action Network via Zapier. We manage suppression lists internally — your existing members don't get re-acquired. Reporting delivered clearly, not buried in dashboards.

Why AHG

The phone number changes everything.

Most digital acquisition stops at email. We capture phone simultaneously — at no additional cost per lead — because that's what separates a list from an organizing operation.

Phone numbers are the bridge to offline. They let your staff make calls, let your organizers do follow-up, and let your campaign run SMS mobilization without an expensive re-acquisition push. We've built the workflow specifically for organizations that measure success by who shows up, not who signed up.

We're also one of the few shops running bilingual Meta campaigns with genuine optimization depth — not just translated copy, but separate form testing, audience segmentation, and CPL tracking by language.
